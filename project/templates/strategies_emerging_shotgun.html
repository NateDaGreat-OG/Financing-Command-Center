Emerging Shotgun Strategy

Emerging Shotgun is a high‑velocity breakout strategy designed to identify early‑stage explosive moves in emerging tickers. It combines:

  • Cycle alignment (short‑term + mid‑term)
  • Volume shock detection
  • Massive news sentiment triggers
  • RL‑based probability scoring
  • Shotgun‑style multi‑signal confirmation

Core Logic

The strategy fires when multiple micro‑signals converge within a tight window, producing a “shotgun burst” of confirmation.

Signals Used

  • Volume Burst Index (VBI)
  • Cycle Momentum Delta (CMD)
  • News Sentiment Spike (NSS)
  • RL Probability > 0.62
  • Breakout Structure Integrity (BSI)

Execution

Positions are taken only when all signals align within a 3‑minute window. Stops and targets are dynamically adjusted using your RL engine.
